# Evidence Index

## Rules

- Evidence IDs are unique and never reused.
- Every item identifies its test execution, source baseline, file identity or checksum, creation date, owner, and retention location.
- Mutable paths without revision or hash are insufficient for release-level conclusions.
- Screenshots support observations but may not prove timing, packet loss, or internal state without corresponding logs or captures.
